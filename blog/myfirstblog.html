Here we go, Where?


Hello, welcome to the first of my hopefully many articles. It really took me some time to get up and running mainly because I was looking for something spectacular to kick off with, but then just today it hit me JUST DO IT (Nike Credits). The first step in any direction is usually the hardest I suppose, one just has to plunge into it though I guess (I mean what's the risk in blogging anyway).


So the main reason for starting my blog is to give myself an outlet of my random thoughts which come to me from time to time, I usually have some interesting insights and sometimes rather awkward scrambled ideologies. This is yet to be seen or read rather; I do hope they don't end up being a "better I had kept that to myself" thing. However more often than less have realised that some of my random scrambled thoughts tend not to be so unique, but I generally think they are very profound (Confucius Philosophical levels). I feel it's worth sharing just in case some like mind person out there happen to catch it and well who knows what will have started. Am taking a journey to the depth of my mind to unrevealed regions of human consciousness and sub-consciousness and daring to bare it all while asking those questions and most probably answer them myself (for the first few I guess), who knows where this will take me, ‘he he he’. Inspire a movement or whatever (wink).


Anyway now that I have started I can now go deeper, the reason why I took me so long is probably going to be my next piece cause I know quite a number of my fellow man suffer from procrastination or INERTIA or comfort zoning, yes let me tackle that in Why It Took So Long (hopefully that won't take me long). So, this seemed easy considering only took me 5 minutes to write, see you on the next one(really hope I don't slack). Drop a comment. [oops]
