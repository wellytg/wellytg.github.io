Here we go, Where?


Hello, welcome to the first of my hopefully many articles. It really took me some time to get up and running mainly because I was looking for something spectacular to kick off with, but then just today it hit me JUST DO IT (Nike Credits). The first step in any direction is usually the hardest I suppose, one just has to plunge into it though I guess (I mean what's the risk in blogging anyway).


So the main reason for starting my blog is to give myself an outlet of my random thoughts which come tome from time to time, I usually have some interesting insights or rather awkward scrambled ideologies and though it yet to be seen or read rather; I may be better of keeping them to myself. However more often than less have realised that some of my random scrambled thoughts tend not to be so unique and at times and do get to be very profound (Confucius Philosophical like), I do feel it's worth sharing just in case some like mind person out may happen to catch it. Am taking a journey to the depth of my mind to unrevealed regions of human consciousness and sub-consciousness and daring to ask those questions and most probably answer them myself, how knows where this will take me, ‘he he he'. May probably spark a serious debate or inspire movement or whatever (wink wink).


Anyway now that I have started I can now go deeper, the reason why I took me so long is probably going to be my next piece cause I know quite a number of my fellow man suffer from INERTIA or procrastination, yes let me tackle that in Why It Took So Long (hopefully that won't take me long). So, this seemed easy considering only took me 5 minutes to write, see you on the next (really wonder how I will see you). Drop a comment. [oops]
